BACI ANNUAL GOODS SNAPSHOT

Source: CEPII BACI HS92, V202601. All 20 sample countries, every year 2000-2024,
both imports and exports: 500 country-years per direction. The supplied annual
composition file is reconciled to the annual product data during the build.

The product CSV files retain their supplied bytes. value_thousand_usd and china_v
are in thousands of current US dollars. Browser data and derived tables multiply
these by 1000. quantity_tonnes is metric tonnes. HS codes must be read as strings
to retain leading zeros. HS2/HS4 values sum the corresponding HS6 values.

For a SINGLE HS6: unit value (USD/tonne) = value (USD) / quantity (tonnes).
Zero or missing quantity produces a missing unit value. Do not add tonnes across
unrelated goods. Unit value is not a pure price index: quality, composition within
an HS6 code, and partner mix may change. No recorded product-year remains missing,
not zero. Aggregate category zeros mean no recorded value within an observed
country-year. The presence of a country-year does not mean every HS6 is observed.

The broad light-consumer basket is research-defined, not an official category:
WITS consumer goods in HS61,62,64 or HS6309, plus low-technology WITS consumer goods
excluding HS01-24,27,30,84,85,87. Only this broad basket is offered in the interface.
Original files retain source classification columns, including historical narrow
flags, for provenance. They are not a second basket offered by the website.
Shares include all merchandise in the denominator, including unclassified codes.
China share for the light basket = China-origin basket value / total basket value.
GDP and population denominators use same-country/year WDI data; missing values are
not filled with another year's observation. See annual country-year coverage CSV.

Top-products table: up to 100 products per country/year/flow/HS level (2,4,6),
ranked by current USD, ties broken by HS code. Each level is a separate ranking.
Shares use ALL merchandise, not just the top 100. Do not add levels together.

Merchandise partner data are annual 2000-2024: 149,043 records, all 20 countries,
both directions and all recorded partners. All 1,000 country/year/flow groups are
present. Partner sums reconcile with annual product totals, including China-origin
imports from product-level china_v. Exports to China and partner concentration are
also annual. Historical territory definitions follow the source; display names
use each partner code's latest recorded name. Services remain 2000-2023.

The annual manifest omits the supplied local computer path but retains release,
years and countries, adds definitions and SHA256 hashes of the four source files.
Older master inventory and coverage tables document the original snapshot; this
annual manifest and annual goods/partner coverage CSVs supersede their BACI coverage.
Other macro, services, FDI and employment source definitions are unchanged.
